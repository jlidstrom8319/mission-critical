# Requests for Information (RFIs)

An RFI asks the design team to clarify the contract documents. It is not a change order, a submittal, or a substitution request — but its answer can lead to one.

## Before writing one

1. Search the documents first: specs, all disciplines' drawings, addenda, ASIs, and prior RFIs. Many RFIs are rejected as "information is in the documents."
2. Confirm the question is the contractor's to ask (not a means-and-methods decision the contractor owns).
3. Combine related questions on the same issue; split unrelated ones.

## RFI content

- **Number / date / required response date** (use the contract's response time, often 7–10 days; shorten only with reason).
- **Subject**: short, specific (`Beam pocket conflict at grid C/4, Level 2`).
- **References**: sheet & detail, spec section & paragraph, photo or markup.
- **Question**: one clear question per item, stating the conflict or missing information factually. *"Detail 5/S-501 shows the beam bearing at EL 112'-6"; 3/A-301 shows the slab at EL 113'-0". Which elevation governs?"*
- **Proposed solution** (optional but encouraged): what the contractor suggests, with sketch.
- **Impact**: potential cost/schedule impact *yes / no / unknown*, and the activity affected and the date the answer is needed to avoid delay.
- **Originator** (sub/trade) and distribution.

## Tone

Neutral and factual. No blame, no claims language, no demands. Do not assume the answer.

## Log fields

RFI #, subject, references, originator, date sent, date required, date answered, ball-in-court, status (draft/open/answered/closed/void), cost impact, schedule impact, resulting document (ASI, bulletin, CO, CCD).

Report on: open RFIs past due, RFIs on critical-path activities, and answers that changed scope (feed to change management).

## Reviewing a response

- Does it answer the question asked?
- Does it change scope, cost, or time? If so, flag it for a change order request within the contract's notice period — RFI answers often say "no cost impact," which the contractor may disagree with in writing.
- Distribute the answer to affected trades and update the conformed set.
