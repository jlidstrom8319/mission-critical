# Construction Subcontracts

Present findings as a Project Engineer's review for the project team. Flag risk and missing terms; do not give legal advice — recommend counsel for anything significant.

## Typical structure

Agreement (parties, price, documents) + General Conditions + exhibits:

| Exhibit | Content |
|---|---|
| Scope of work | Inclusions/exclusions by spec section & drawing; general obligations |
| Contract documents list | Sheets, specs, addenda, with dates |
| Schedule | Milestones, durations, look-ahead participation |
| Pricing | Schedule of values, alternates, unit prices, allowances |
| Insurance & bonds | Coverages, limits, additional insured, waivers |
| Safety / site rules | Site-specific requirements |
| Billing & lien waivers | Forms, cut-off dates |
| Closeout | O&M, warranties, as-builts, attic stock |

Common forms: AIA A401, ConsensusDocs 750, or the contractor's own form.

## Review checklist

1. **Flow-down**: prime contract obligations to the owner (schedule, warranty, insurance, notice periods, dispute process) are passed to the sub at least as strictly.
2. **Scope**: matches the leveled bid and scope review; every bidder qualification is accepted, rejected, or priced. No item belongs to two subs or none.
3. **Price & payment**: lump sum vs. unit price; pay-when-paid vs. pay-if-paid (enforceability varies by state); retainage % and reduction; payment timing.
4. **Changes**: written authorization required, pricing method (markups for OH&P), notice deadlines for claims, directed work.
5. **Schedule & delay**: obligation to follow the project schedule, acceleration, liquidated damages flow-down, no-damages-for-delay clauses.
6. **Insurance & indemnity**: limits meet the prime contract; additional insured on primary & non-contributory basis; waiver of subrogation; indemnity scope (anti-indemnity statutes vary by state).
7. **Bonds**: performance and payment bond requirements and amounts.
8. **Warranty**: duration and start date match the prime contract (typically from substantial completion).
9. **Termination**: for cause (notice and cure period) and for convenience (payment terms).
10. **Disputes**: process, venue, governing law, consistent with the prime contract.
11. **Compliance**: prevailing wage, certified payroll, DBE/MBE, safety programs, licensing.

## Output

- A table: clause, what it says (with section number), issue/risk, recommendation.
- Rank issues High/Medium/Low.
- Mark which issues are commercial (PM can resolve) vs. legal (send to counsel).
- Never insert personal information about individuals beyond what is needed for party identification.
